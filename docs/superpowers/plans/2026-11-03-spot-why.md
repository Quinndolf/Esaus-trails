# Why This Spot Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** After Analyze area, tapping one of Esau's numbered sit spots opens a card that teaches why he picked it: wind and scent, air movement (thermals), the ground, which way to face and how to walk in. The tutorial mentions it.

**Architecture:** A pure `spotWhy(spot, reading, opts)` in `src/esau/spotWhy.ts` turns a `SitSpot` plus the conditions model's `PointReading` for that spot and hour into labelled lines. `src/home/SpotWhyCard.tsx` holds a tiny zustand store (`useSpotWhy`, selected rank) and renders the card for the slider's hour. The map markers in `src/home/MapExtras.tsx` become buttons that select their rank.

**Tech Stack:** React 18, zustand, Vitest (jsdom).

**Spec:** Quinn's ask, relayed 2026-11-03: "when it analyzes and Esau gives us his top three spots ... you should have the ability to click it and give a reason reasonings for that pick", and the tutorial should mention it.

## Global Constraints
- Reads only land, wind and weather; never the hunter's marks, photos or tracks.
- Free; no new services. Works with no Anthropic key (pure, on-device).
- Beginner language, short lines, normal-size buttons.

## Review Focus
- Scent drifting toward where the hunter faces must say the spot is weak this hour, not "good".
- Swirling scent must not claim a direction.
- A spot outside the read ground (no reading) still shows the pick reason, without inventing wind or terrain.
- Moving the hour slider while the card is open updates the card; a new analysis without that rank closes it.
- Squirrel and turkey spots use their own words (food trees, roost, open ground), not "trail".
